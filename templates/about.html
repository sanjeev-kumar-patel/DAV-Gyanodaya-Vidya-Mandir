Our Journey

1886

DAV Movement Begins

The very first Dayanand Anglo Vedic (DAV) School was established in Lahore, beginning over a century of educational excellence across India.

1990s

Narsingarh Expansion

DAV Gyanodaya Vidya Mandir was officially set up to cater to the growing industrial, suburban, and rural population of the Damoh district.

2023

Excellence in Digital Literacy

Rolled out state-of-the-art interactive smart classrooms, advanced computer training systems, and coding modules for classes VI-XII.

2025

National Recognition

Our Class X students hit record-breaking scores in CBSE board examinations with an outstanding 98.6% passing percentile average.

Core Values

Vedic Heritage

Academic Rigor

Modern Vision
