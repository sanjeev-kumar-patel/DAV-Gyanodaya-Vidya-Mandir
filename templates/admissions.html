Admission Process

A simple, transparent 5-step process to join our institution.

1

Enquiry

Visit the school office or call for information about available classes and admission criteria.

2

Application

Fill out the application form available at the school counter along with the registration fee.

3

Entrance Test

Students appear for a grade-appropriate assessment to evaluate academic readiness.

4

Interview

An interaction with parents and the student to understand backgrounds and expectations.

5

Enrollment

Submit required documents and pay fees to confirm your child's admission.

Required Documents

  • ✓ Birth Certificate (Original + Photocopy)
  • ✓ Transfer Certificate (TC) from Previous School
  • ✓ 4 Passport-size Photographs
  • ✓ Aadhar Card (Student + Parent)
  • ✓ Previous Year's Report Card
  • ✓ Address Proof (Ration Card / Utility Bill)
