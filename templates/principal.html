Message from the Principal

"Our focus is on molding students into responsible citizens with a blend of Vedic heritage and modern technology. We provide a safe and intellectually challenging environment to empower students academically and creatively. Education is not merely about textbooks — it is about building character, instilling values, and nurturing dreams."

At DAV Gyanodaya Vidya Mandir, we believe that every child carries infinite potential. Our dedicated faculty, comprehensive curriculum, and value-based education system work together to unlock this potential. We emphasize holistic growth through academics, sports, yoga, arts, and community service.

I warmly invite parents and guardians to visit our campus, experience our learning environment, and join us in this wonderful journey of nurturing the leaders of tomorrow.

— Mr. R. P. Kurmwanshi
